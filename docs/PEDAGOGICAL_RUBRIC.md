# Pedagogical Evaluation Framework & Rubrics

## 1. Revised Bloom's Taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001)

IntelliGrade maps each examination question to one of the six cognitive process dimensions:

| Level | Dimension | Cognitive Process | Characteristic Action Verbs | Typical Exam Prompts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Remember** | Retrieve relevant knowledge from long-term memory | *define, list, identify, recall, name, state, match, label* | "State the first law of thermodynamics." |
| **2** | **Understand** | Construct meaning from instructional messages (interpret, exemplify, classify, summarize, infer, compare, explain) | *explain, describe, summarize, interpret, clarify, discuss, classify* | "Explain how DNS resolves a domain name to an IP address." |
| **3** | **Apply** | Carry out or use a procedure in a given situation (execute or implement) | *calculate, solve, compute, implement, demonstrate, derive, operate* | "Calculate the resistance in a parallel circuit given R1=10Ω and R2=20Ω." |
| **4** | **Analyze** | Break material into constituent parts and determine how parts relate to one another and to an overall structure | *compare, contrast, differentiate, distinguish, examine, categorize, diagnose* | "Compare and contrast SQL and NoSQL database models under write-heavy workloads." |
| **5** | **Evaluate** | Make judgments based on criteria and standards (check and critique) | *critique, justify, defend, appraise, judge, rate, assess, prioritize* | "Critique the ethical implications of using predictive AI in judicial sentencing." |
| **6** | **Create** | Put elements together to form a novel, coherent whole or make an original product (generate, plan, or produce) | *design, formulate, construct, develop, compose, invent, architect, synthesize* | "Design a fault-tolerant, air-gapped distributed ledger for electronic voting." |

---

## 2. Objective Difficulty Scoring Rubric (1.0 - 10.0)

Difficulty is computed by combining:
1. **Cognitive Tier Base**:
   - Level 1 (Remember): 1.5 – 3.0
   - Level 2 (Understand): 3.0 – 4.5
   - Level 3 (Apply): 4.5 – 6.5
   - Level 4 (Analyze): 6.0 – 7.5
   - Level 5 (Evaluate): 7.0 – 8.8
   - Level 6 (Create): 8.0 – 10.0
2. **Syntactic and Domain Complexity Modifiers**:
   - Question token count and nested clauses (+0.4 to +0.8)
   - Mathematical / symbolic operators present (+0.6)
   - Multi-variable conditional qualifiers ("assuming", "given that", "constraint") (+0.7)

## 3. Exam Paper Balance Health Index (0 - 100%)

An ideal comprehensive university examination follows a balanced distribution:
- **Foundational Recall (Level 1-2)**: 25% – 35%
- **Procedural Application (Level 3)**: 30% – 40%
- **Higher-Order Thinking (Level 4-6)**: 30% – 45%

### Assessment Status Categories:
- **Optimal Balance (80 - 100%)**: Robust balance across foundational knowledge and higher-order critical evaluation.
- **Moderate Balance (60 - 79%)**: Acceptable for introductory or survey courses, slight bias towards lower or higher levels.
- **Rote Recall Warning (< 60%)**: Over-indexed on rote memory (Levels 1–2 > 70%), failing to assess analytical and evaluative skills.
- **Excessive Cognitive Load (< 60%)**: Over-indexed on Level 6 design problems without establishing foundational knowledge.
